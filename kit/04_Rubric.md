# Learn2Think — Thinking Quality Rubric (v4)

Each dimension is scored on four levels (display names as of D2; less grade-like, still ordered):

1. **Starting**
2. **Developing**
3. **Solid**
4. **Strong**

Cases score only the dimensions they list.

| Dimension | 1 Starting | 2 Developing | 3 Solid | 4 Strong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Problem Framing** | Restates the request or jumps to a fix | Narrower, but metric, scope or decision still vague | Specific, bounded, decision-oriented | Reframes the request when the real problem differs, and explains why |
| **Questioning** | Few or generic questions | Relevant but poorly sequenced | Targets the biggest uncertainties first | Surfaces hidden assumptions and stakeholder conflicts |
| **Structuring** | Unstructured list | Structure with gaps or overlaps | Fit-for-purpose structure tied to the goal | Chooses the structure for this problem; knows where it breaks |
| **Assumptions** | None surfaced | Named, not tested | Named and ranked by risk | States what must be true and how to test it cheaply |
| **Research & Evidence** | Opinion as evidence | Evidence of uneven quality | Right method; weighs evidence quality | Seeks disconfirming evidence; knows when evidence is enough |
| **Reasoning** | Conclusion doesn't follow | Mostly follows, with gaps | Clear chain from evidence to conclusion | Handles alternative explanations and confounders explicitly |
| **Alternatives & Trade-offs** | One option | Options listed, trade-offs shallow | Meaningful options with explicit trade-offs | Includes second-order and stakeholder effects |
| **Judgment** | False certainty, or refuses to decide | Decides, with confidence mismatched to evidence | Defensible decision with calibrated confidence | Says what would change the decision; plans for reversibility |
| **Communication** | Hard to follow | Organized, but buries the answer | Answer-first, concise, fits the audience | Handles objections; communicates uncertainty well |
| **Reflection** | None | Describes what happened | Names specific weaknesses in own reasoning | Extracts a transferable lesson |

## Emphasis by stage

| Stage | Dimensions emphasized |
|---|---|
| **Understand / Investigate** | Framing, Questioning, Structuring, Assumptions, Research & Evidence |
| **Decide** | The above + Reasoning, Alternatives & Trade-offs, Judgment |
| **Deliver, Specialist, Capstones** | All ten |

## Who evaluates what

| Evaluator | Role |
|---|---|
| **AI** | Checks structure (assumptions, alternatives, evidence, contradictions, missed factors) and suggests a level with reasons. Never gives a single final score. |
| **Benchmark** | Compares the learner's reasoning path with the case's model answer and its accepted alternatives. |
| **Human** | Judges quality of judgment, domain fit, novel reasoning and communication. Required for integrated challenges and capstones. |
| **Learner** | Self-scores Reflection before seeing feedback. |
