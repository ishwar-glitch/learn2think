# Learn2Think Curriculum v4.2 (Lean Design, launch scope: 4 tracks)

> **Launch scope:** BA, DA, PM, PD plus the career layer (`06`). **SC and SD are post-launch:** their specialist modules (S9, S10, S13, S14), track cases, capstones and U-IN-5 are not built. They stay listed below so the design is not lost.

## 1. The outcome
> A learner can take a vague, real-world business problem and produce a **well-researched, defensible recommendation**: asking the right questions, choosing the right research method, reasoning from evidence, deciding under uncertainty and communicating clearly. They prove it through a **portfolio of solved cases**, not certificates.

Everything in this curriculum exists to build that outcome. If content doesn't serve it, it doesn't get made.

**Learners:**
- **Students:** build professional thinking before they have experience.
- **Career switchers:** translate their existing experience into a new role.

Both use the same content. They differ in case variant and starting point.

## 2. The Thinking Loop (the backbone)
**Ask → Frame → Structure → Research → Analyze → Decide → Communicate → Reflect**

Every case challenge walks through this loop. Every core module strengthens one or two of its stages.

## 3. Design rules that keep production lean

| # | Rule | What it replaces from v3 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Three files per module:** a Concept Set (concept blocks with quizzes), a Mastery Check, and a short Module Report | 9 pieces per unit |
| 2 | **Repeatable experiences are platform features,** specified once in `05_Platform_Features.md`: AI Opponent, Thinking Record, confidence calibration, diagnostic placement | AI Opponent scripts and Thinking Record prompts written per unit |
| 3 | **One Company Universe:** ~9 fictional companies, profiled once and reused in every case | New companies per unit; separate Case Registry |
| 4 | **Cases live in a shared library.** Each case has Student (scaffolded) and Professional variants, so it doubles as the guided exercise | Separate guided exercises, challenges and case packs |
| 5 | **Tracks are paths, not courses:** core modules + 2–4 specialist modules + 4 track cases + 1 capstone (4 tracks at launch) | 3 new units per track, plus a Bridge |
| 6 | **Difficulty comes from depth and case variant,** not extra units | 4 levels of separate units |
| 7 | **Mastery Checks double as the diagnostic:** career switchers take them up front to test out of modules | Separate diagnostic content |
| 8 | **AI auditing is a quiz item type (`AUDIT`),** at least one per module | Separate AI Check activity per unit |

**Production estimate (launch):** 20 modules (10 core + 10 specialist) × 2 content files + ~34 cases + 8 company profiles + career-layer files (T8–T10, ~14) ≈ **95 files**. v3 needed ≈ 290.

## 4. Structure

```
CORE — 10 modules in 4 stages, with an integrated challenge after stages 2, 3 and 4
        │
SPECIALIST POOL — 14 modules; each track draws the ones it needs
        │
TRACK PATHS — BA · DA · PM · PD   (SC · SD post-launch)
        │
PORTFOLIO — every solved case + Thinking Record
        │
CAREER LAYER — Story Bank, Interview Lab, JD Decoder, Tool Labs (see `06`, F12–F15)
```

## 5. Core modules (everyone takes these)
Each module has 5–7 concept blocks, 1 library case, and 1 Mastery Check. Concepts are **owned** by exactly one module: introduced there, and only applied elsewhere.

### Stage 1 — UNDERSTAND
**Depth:** concept checks D1–D3; case D3.

| Module | Owns |
|---|---|
| **M1 How Businesses Work** | customers & value proposition · business models · revenue & cost structure · P&L basics · unit economics (contribution margin, CAC, LTV, payback) · value chain · competition basics · pricing basics |
| **M2 Problems & Questions** | problem vs symptom / request / solution · objective vs constraint · facts vs assumptions · known vs unknown · signal vs noise · scope · identifying stakeholders · problem statements · question types (clarifying, probing, assumption-testing, "so what?", "compared to what?") · question sequencing |

### Stage 2 — INVESTIGATE
**Depth:** concept checks D1–D3; cases D3.

| Module | Owns |
|---|---|
| **M3 Structuring Problems** | first principles & "what must be true?" · inversion · necessary vs sufficient · decomposition & issue trees · MECE · hypothesis trees · root cause (5 Whys, fishbone) · Pareto · prioritization (impact × effort) · when a framework becomes bureaucracy |
| **M4 Research Methods** | research questions · primary vs secondary · desk research & source credibility · interviews & non-leading questions · survey basics · qual vs quant · sampling · research plans · "when is enough information enough?" |
| **M5 Evidence & Causation** | testable hypotheses · evidence quality · falsification · correlation vs causation · confounding · base rates · evidence biases (selection, survivorship, confirmation) · intuitive Bayesian updating · stating confidence |
| **M6 Numbers & Metrics** | ratios, rates & growth · mean vs median & distributions · driver & KPI trees · funnels · cohorts · metric types (leading/lagging, vanity/actionable) · estimation & market sizing · probability & expected value |

**Integrated Challenge IC1 — The Broken Business.** Frame → structure → hypothesize → research plan. No solution allowed.

### Stage 3 — DECIDE
**Depth:** concept checks D2–D4; cases D4.

| Module | Owns |
|---|---|
| **M7 Decisions & Trade-offs** | risk vs uncertainty · reversible vs irreversible · opportunity cost · decision criteria · decision trees · scenarios · pre-mortem · trade-off curves & the binding constraint · classic tensions (cost/quality, speed/reliability, growth/profitability, automation/human judgment) · "what would change my mind?" |
| **M8 Systems & Stakeholders** | feedback loops · stocks & flows · delays & bottlenecks · leverage points · second-order effects · stakeholder mapping · incentives & principal–agent · local vs global optimization · responsible decisions (privacy, fairness, customer harm) |

**Integrated Challenge IC2 — The System Under Pressure.** Several simultaneous problems: what is really happening, what to solve first, and a defensible decision.

### Stage 4 — DELIVER
**Depth:** concept checks D3–D4; cases D4.

| Module | Owns |
|---|---|
| **M9 Judgment & AI** | heuristics & judgment biases (anchoring, overconfidence, sunk cost) · calibration · decision quality vs outcome quality · intuition vs evidence · AI as challenger/researcher · AI failure modes · auditing an AI analysis · human accountability |
| **M10 Communicate & Execute** | answer-first structure · decision narrative (Problem → Evidence → Insight → Options → Trade-off → Recommendation → Risk) · memos & executive summaries · communicating uncertainty · handling objections · influence without authority · execution plan, success metrics & review checkpoints |

**Integrated Challenge IC3 — The Master Challenge.** The full loop on a messy cross-functional case, including an AI-generated analysis to audit and a live AI Opponent defense.

## 9. Assessment
- Scored with the Thinking Quality Rubric (`04_Rubric.md`). At launch: learner self-review checklist + platform checks + model-answer comparison; capstones get optional founder review (F7). Live-LLM scoring is a later upgrade.
- Confidence is tagged on every quiz item and feeds calibration (M9).
- Portfolio = solved cases + Thinking Records + lab artifacts (private by default; F15).
- Progress is a weekly goal and a skill map, never a streak or XP (F16).

## 10. Career layer (launch)
Built once on the platform (features F9–F16, templates T8–T10); no extra modules: Day-1 micro-case, Daily Rep, Personal Problem mode, Story Bank, Experience Translator, Interview Lab, JD Decoder, Tool Fluency Labs, public portfolio. Positioning: evidence of how you reason, never "job-ready". Post-launch: L1/L2 modules, Live Cases, peer pods, employer view (see `06`).
