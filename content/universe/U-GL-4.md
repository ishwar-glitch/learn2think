# U-GL-4 — Northgate Home (e-commerce retailer)
**Context:** GLOBAL   **Currency:** USD   **Industry:** E-commerce retailer with its own warehouses (home goods)   **Name status:** fictional; trademark check needed [VERIFY]

## Story
Northgate Home sells furniture, kitchenware and home decor online. Unlike marketplaces, it buys its own stock and ships from four warehouses it operates. Founded in 2012, it grew profitably at first. Rising shipping costs, returns and heavy November–December peaks now squeeze a thin margin.

## Business model
Northgate buys goods from suppliers, stores them, and sells at a markup on its site and app. Its costs are the goods themselves, warehouse and shipping, and marketing. Returns and late deliveries hurt both cost and reputation.

## Canon facts
1. Founded 2012, headquartered in Columbus. About 2,900 employees; 1,900 work in warehouses.
2. Four warehouses, each serving a region.
3. Revenue $640 million a year from 8.0 million orders, so average order value $80.
4. About 42,000 products (SKUs). Inventory turns about 5.1 times a year.
5. Gross margin 38% (before fulfilment and shipping), so about $243 million.
6. Fulfilment (picking, packing) about $6.2 per order plus outbound shipping about $8.4 per order: $14.6 per order, about $117 million a year (18.25% of revenue).
7. Marketing 12% of revenue (about $77 million). Technology and general costs about 5% (about $32 million).
8. Operating profit (EBITDA) about $17.6 million, about 2.75% of revenue (243.2 − 116.8 − 76.8 − 32).
9. Return rate 14% of orders. On-time delivery 93%.
10. November and December bring 31% of annual revenue; warehouse hiring peaks then.
11. Customer satisfaction (survey) 4.1 out of 5; delivery and damage are the top complaints.

## Strengths and weaknesses (case seeds)
- **Strengths:** control of stock and delivery; wide range; loyal repeat buyers.
- **Weaknesses:** thin margin; high returns; peak-season strain; shipping-cost exposure; large inventory tied up.
- **Seeds:** return-rate reduction; free-shipping threshold; peak-season staffing; late-delivery root cause; supplier range decisions.
