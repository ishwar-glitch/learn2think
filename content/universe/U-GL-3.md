# U-GL-3 — Fixnest (home-services marketplace)
**Context:** GLOBAL   **Currency:** USD   **Industry:** Home-services marketplace   **Name status:** fictional; trademark check needed [VERIFY]

## Story
Fixnest connects households with independent cleaners, plumbers, electricians, painters and appliance repairers. Founded in 2015, it operates in 30 metro areas across three countries. It has two customers to keep happy: homeowners who want reliable work, and professionals who want steady jobs. Both sometimes trade off against each other, and some pairs quietly move off the platform after the first job.

## Business model
A customer books a job in the app. The platform matches a professional, holds payment, and releases it after the job, keeping a commission. Growth needs both more demand and enough good professionals in every city.

## Canon facts
1. Founded 2015, headquartered in Chicago. 30 metro areas in three countries. About 1,100 employees.
2. About 620,000 completed jobs a month across categories: cleaning 38%, plumbing 17%, electrical 15%, appliance repair 14%, painting 10%, other 6%.
3. Average booking value $118, so gross booking value about $73 million a month, about $878 million a year.
4. Commission (take rate) 22%, so revenue about $193 million a year.
5. About 46,000 active professionals; about 13.5 jobs per professional a month.
6. Customer rating 4.6 out of 5. 12% of bookings are cancelled.
7. 27% of customers book a second job within three months. An estimated 18% of repeat jobs move off the platform ("leakage") [VERIFY method].
8. New professionals go through a background check and skills test; only 41% of applicants pass.
9. Customer support handles about 90,000 contacts a month; about 3% of jobs get a dispute.
10. Peak demand is spring (cleaning, painting) and winter (plumbing, heating).
