# U-IN-3 — Aarambh Learning (test-prep edtech)
**Context:** IN   **Currency:** ₹   **Industry:** Test-prep edtech (app plus live classes)   **Name status:** fictional; trademark check needed [VERIFY]

## Story
Aarambh Learning started in 2016 in Hyderabad, helping students in classes 11–12 prepare for engineering and medical entrance exams. It added adult courses for bank and government exams in 2020. Free content brings in millions of students; a smaller number pay for live batches and test series. Parents ask hard questions about results and refunds.

## Business model
Students sign up free and get sample classes and quizzes. Paid plans (annual) give live batches, recorded lessons, doubt-solving and test series. Revenue is subscriptions; the largest costs are marketing and teachers.

## Canon facts
1. Founded 2016, headquartered in Hyderabad.
2. 6.2 million registered free users; 310,000 active paid subscribers (5.0% of free users).
3. Average net revenue per paid subscriber ₹14,000 a year (after discounts), so revenue about ₹434 crore a year.
4. Renewal rate 41%. Refund rate 9% of new sales.
5. About 900 teachers: 600 full-time, 300 part-time. Live batches run in Hindi, English and Telugu.
6. Costs as a share of revenue: teachers 32% (about ₹139 crore), marketing 38% (about ₹165 crore), content and technology 14% (about ₹61 crore), support and operations 10% (about ₹43 crore). Operating profit 6% (about ₹26 crore).
7. Live-class attendance averages 34% of enrolled students; recorded-lesson completion 22%.
8. Two product lines: school-exam prep (about 70% of revenue) and adult exam prep (about 30%).
9. Peak season for sales is October to January (about 55% of annual sales).
10. Student support: a doubt-solving team answers within 4 hours on average. Complaint rate about 2% of subscribers a month.
