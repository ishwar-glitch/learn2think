# U-GL-1 — Ticketwise (B2B helpdesk software)
**Context:** GLOBAL   **Currency:** USD   **Industry:** B2B helpdesk software (SaaS)   **Name status:** fictional; trademark check needed [VERIFY]

## Story
Ticketwise sells software that lets companies handle customer questions by email, chat and phone in one place. Founded in 2014, it grew by selling to small and mid-size teams. Bigger rivals and AI assistants now pressure it. In 2025 it launched "AutoReply", which drafts answers for support agents, and the company is deciding how far to push AI.

## Business model
Customers pay per support agent per month. Plans: Starter $29, Growth $79, Enterprise by contract. Revenue is recurring, so the key questions are growth in seats, churn (customers leaving), and expansion from existing customers.

## Canon facts
1. Founded 2014, headquartered in Toronto. About 820 employees.
2. 9,800 paying customer companies and about 310,000 paid agent seats (average about 32 seats per company).
3. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) $148 million, so about $15,100 per customer and about $477 per seat a year (about $40 a month blended).
4. Gross margin 78%.
5. Net revenue retention 104%. Yearly logo churn (customers who leave) 14%.
6. Customer acquisition cost (CAC) payback about 19 months.
7. The platform handles about 41 million support tickets a month.
8. AutoReply (AI drafts) launched 2025. Agents use a suggested draft on about 22% of replies. It is sold as an add-on.
9. Support quality score (customer satisfaction on tickets) averages 87%.
10. Two main competitors: a large enterprise vendor and a low-price challenger. Ticketwise wins mostly on ease of use.

## Strengths and weaknesses (case seeds)
- **Strengths:** easy to adopt; good small-team fit; strong data on tickets.
- **Weaknesses:** churn among small customers; AI drafts are trusted on only some replies; discounting to win deals; enterprise features lag.
- **Seeds:** AI-support feature launch (PM); churn root cause; pricing change; AI answers that are wrong (audit); requirements from conflicting customers.
