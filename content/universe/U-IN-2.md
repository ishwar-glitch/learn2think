# U-IN-2 — Tarazu Credit (digital lender)
**Context:** IN   **Currency:** ₹   **Industry:** Digital NBFC (personal and small-business loans)   **Name status:** fictional; trademark check needed [VERIFY]

## Story
Tarazu Credit began in 2018 in Mumbai as an app-only lender for salaried people who were ignored by banks. It added small-business loans in 2021. Applications are fast, but most applicants either get rejected or drop off during identity checks, and collections teams chase a rising share of late payers.

## Business model
Tarazu lends its own funds (borrowed from banks and investors) and earns interest and processing fees. Profit depends on approving good borrowers, keeping the cost of funds low and limiting bad loans. It is a non-bank lender registered with the central bank [VERIFY exact status and rules].

## Canon facts
1. Founded 2018, headquartered in Mumbai. About 1,450 employees, 380 of them in collections.
2. Two products. Personal loans: ₹25,000 to ₹5 lakh, 6–36 months. Small-business loans: ₹2 lakh to ₹25 lakh, 12–48 months.
3. Loan book (money currently lent out) ₹3,200 crore: personal ₹2,000 crore, small business ₹1,200 crore.
4. Monthly disbursals ₹380 crore: personal ₹250 crore (about 20,000 loans of average ₹1.25 lakh), small business ₹130 crore (about 1,625 loans of average ₹8 lakh). Total about 21,625 loans a month.
5. Monthly funnel: about 200,000 applications started, 124,000 submitted (38% abandon before submitting, most at the identity-check step), about 29,800 approved (24% of submitted), 21,625 disbursed (about 73% of approved).
6. Yield on the book about 24% a year, so interest and fee income about ₹768 crore a year.
7. Cost of funds 10.5%, so about ₹336 crore; net interest income about ₹432 crore.
8. Credit cost (loans written off) about 4.5% of the book, about ₹144 crore. Operating costs about ₹190 crore. Profit before tax about ₹98 crore (432 − 144 − 190).
9. Late payments: 6.8% of the book is 30+ days late; 2.9% is 90+ days late.
10. Median time from submitted application to money in account: 26 hours for personal loans, 4 days for small-business loans.
11. Identity check uses digital KYC (video and document checks) [VERIFY current regulation].

## Strengths and weaknesses (case seeds)
- **Strengths:** fast decisions; good data on repayments; loyal repeat borrowers.
- **Weaknesses:** heavy drop-off at identity check; a model that rejects thin-file applicants; rising late payments among newer borrowers; long small-business process.
- **Seeds:** loan-origination transformation (BA capstone); first-time applicant experience (PD capstone); process failure; conflicting requirements between risk and product; automation opportunity in verification.
