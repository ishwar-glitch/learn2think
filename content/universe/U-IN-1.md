# U-IN-1 — Pinlo (quick commerce)
**Context:** IN   **Currency:** ₹   **Industry:** Quick commerce   **Name status:** fictional; trademark check needed [VERIFY]

## Story
Pinlo started in 2021 in Bengaluru with one promise: groceries and daily needs at your door in about 15 minutes. It runs from small "dark stores" (warehouses closed to the public) placed inside neighbourhoods. Growth was fast, but every order still costs almost as much to deliver as the company earns from it. The board wants unit economics to turn positive before the next funding round.

## Business model
Customers order on the app. A dark store picks and packs the order, and a gig rider delivers it. Pinlo earns from product margin, supplier commissions, delivery and handling fees, and brand advertising. It pays riders per order plus incentives.

## Canon facts
1. Founded 2021, headquartered in Bengaluru.
2. 140 dark stores: Bengaluru 60, Hyderabad 30, Pune 20, six tier-2 cities 30 (5 each: Mysuru, Coimbatore, Nagpur, Indore, Kochi, Jaipur).
3. Average 1,100 orders per store per day, so 154,000 orders a day and about 56.2 million a year.
4. Average order value (AOV) ₹420. Gross merchandise value (GMV) about ₹2,361 crore a year.
5. Revenue per order ₹80 (about 19% of AOV), so about ₹450 crore a year.
6. Cost per order ₹82: rider ₹38, picking and packing ₹12, store rent and utilities ₹14, packaging ₹6, payment fees ₹5, wastage and refunds ₹7. Contribution per order is therefore −₹2. Stores older than 12 months with 1,400+ orders a day reach +₹4.
7. Brand promise: "in 15 minutes or less". Network average actual delivery time is 14 minutes; evening peak (7–9 pm) is slower.
8. 9,800 partner riders, about 70 rostered per store on average, paid per order.
9. 5,500 products (SKUs) per store. 1.9 million monthly active customers, about 2.5 orders each per month.
10. App rating 4.4 out of 5. Complaints are about 3.2% of orders.
11. Corporate costs (tech, people, marketing) about ₹133 crore a year, so the total cash burn is about ₹144 crore a year (₹12 crore a month).

## Strengths and weaknesses (case seeds)
- **Strengths:** dense stores in Bengaluru; a strong delivery brand; good data on orders.
- **Weaknesses:** negative contribution per order; evening peaks strain picking, not just riders; substitutions and missing items drive complaints; managers ask for capacity before diagnosing.
- **Seeds:** late deliveries at one store; missing-item complaints; paid subscription tier; discounting habit; rider incentives.
