Notes for this screen

What it is for

The learner asks a stakeholder questions from a menu. Answers use only case facts. Good questions unlock exhibits. It is not a free-text chat.

Decided (D1)

#17 The menu shows 4 questions at a time, refreshed after each ask.
#18 The results page lists the most useful questions the learner asked.

Spec notes

  • Menu of 10–15 questions per stakeholder in the real case; 8 shown here. Kinds are useful, irrelevant or leading. Kind is not displayed to the learner.
  • Decided #17: the menu shows 4 questions at a time, refreshed after each ask, to keep the struggle. Decided #18: the results page (11) lists your most useful questions.
  • Asks are limited (6 in Professional, 10 in Student). The counter is always visible; when it hits zero the menu closes.
  • Leading questions get an honest answer that shows the trap; irrelevant ones get a polite non-answer, never a hint.
  • Useful questions can unlock an exhibit (a chip appears, and the exhibit becomes available on screen 10).
  • Free-text questions are the paid 'Live AI' upgrade later; the menu design carries over.
