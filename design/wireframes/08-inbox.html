Notes for this screen

What it is for

Frames every case as a job assignment. The learner opens a message from a Company Universe character and chooses Student or Professional.

Decided (D1)

#15 Choose the variant first; switching later resets hints and asks.
#16 The Professional variant is shown locked (Plus) on Free.

Spec notes

  • Inbox is framing only; no new content. Sender, subject and deadline come from the case file.
  • Student variant: clear prompt, exhibits provided, hint ladder available. Professional: vague manager message, exhibits on request, no hints.
  • Case workspace tabs (Brief, Ask, Data, Draft) appear once the case is opened; screens 09–11.
  • Promotions (Intern to Analyst) are optional framing and are drawn on screen 14, not here.
