Learn2Think · D3 mock-ups

Batch D3 · full redesign

19 screens, one calm system.

Phone-first, with a real desktop layout. Light and dark themes (the moon button switches). Every screen keeps its D1 decisions in the Notes drawer, top right.

All case text is a sample (a quick-commerce company, U-IN-1). The real CASE-M2 arrives in batch C2. Numbers are illustrative.

How to review

  1. Start at 01 and use the arrows in the top strip to walk the flow to 19.
  2. Try it on your phone and on your computer; resize to see the desktop layout.
  3. Try the quiz (06), stakeholder chat (09), data room (10), hint ladder (11), Opponent (12) and tree builder (07).
  4. Look for: anything that feels like a game, school or chatbot; any job promise; too much on one screen.

Screens

01Landing and waitlistFirst contact. Explains the idea in 10 seconds, lets a visitor try the Day-1 case without signing up, and (before launch) joins the waitlist.02OnboardingTurns four answers into a plan and a weekly goal, without a long form. Runs after the Day-1 case and sign-up (case, sign-up, onboarding, home).03Day-1 case (first 15 minutes)The first win: ask questions, write a 3-sentence recommendation, get challenged once, and leave with a first Thinking Record.04Home and skill mapOrients the learner around what to practise next. The skill map shows rubric level per Thinking Loop stage; there are no points.05Concept blockTeaches one idea the struggle-first way: Situation, Attempt, Reveal, Check, Go Deeper.06Quiz with confidenceShows the interaction patterns used for checks and Daily Reps, so the product is not all multiple choice. Every item asks for confidence first.07Tree builderLets learners build an issue or driver tree by placing pieces, then checks it for overlap (MECE) and asks 'so what?'.08Case inbox and briefFrames every case as a job assignment. The learner opens a message from a Company Universe character and chooses Student or Professional.09Stakeholder chat (scripted)The learner asks a stakeholder questions from a menu. Answers use only case facts. Good questions unlock exhibits. It is not a free-text chat.10Data roomWhere exhibits live. In the Student variant they are all visible; in the Professional variant the learner must ask for each by name, and the request is logged.11Submission, hints and self-reviewWhere the learner writes the recommendation, can climb the hint ladder, checks their own work against a checklist, and then sees the model answer.12Scripted OpponentStress-tests the recommendation with two or three persona challenges, then requires a revision and a written 'what changed and why'.13Thinking RecordThe artifact every case leaves behind: 12 fixed fields showing how the learner reasoned. Fields shown depend on the stage.14Portfolio and evidenceShows what the learner has demonstrated, private by default, with an optional public page. It states facts, never a verdict or a 'ready' score.15Interview LabTimed interview practice from a question bank. The learner answers aloud (recorded on the device only) or in text, then self-rates against a checklist and a model answer.16Story BankTurns a Thinking Record into interview stories, using only what the learner wrote and confirmed.17JD DecoderThe learner pastes a job description and sees which skills they have evidenced, which are gaps, and which the platform does not cover.18Pricing and paywallExplains Free vs Plus plainly, and shows the soft paywall a learner meets at Stage 2.19Personal Problem worksheetThe learner brings a real work or interview problem and works it through the Thinking Loop by answering prompts, ending in a Thinking Record.
