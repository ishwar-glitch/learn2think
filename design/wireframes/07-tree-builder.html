Notes for this screen

What it is for

Lets learners build an issue or driver tree by placing pieces, then checks it for overlap (MECE) and asks 'so what?'.

Decided (D1)

#13 Tap-and-place only at launch; no free typing of nodes.
#14 Appears in M3 and M6, plus an optional, ungraded tool inside cases.

Spec notes

  • Tap a chip in the tray, then tap a slot to place it. Tap a filled slot to take it back. Try placing 'App downloads' and press Check.
  • Tray includes distractors (relevant-looking but off-topic). Placing one is flagged with a hatch pattern and a reason.
  • The MECE check names overlaps (two branches that count the same thing) and gaps; it does not give a score.
  • The 'so what?' box asks what the learner would do differently if each branch turned out to be the cause.
  • Decided #13: tap-and-place only at launch, no free typing of nodes. Decided #14: appears in M3 and M6, plus as an optional, ungraded tool inside cases.
  • On a phone the tree scrolls down; on desktop it can sit side by side with the tray.
