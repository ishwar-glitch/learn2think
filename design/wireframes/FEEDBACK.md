The founder's answers are in `../D1_decisions.md`. Each screen shows its own decisions in the Notes drawer.

## 01: Landing and waitlist
- #1 Headline stays: "Practise how you think, not what you memorised." A second line is A/B tested after launch.
- #2 The Day-1 case is playable before sign-up; progress is saved on register.

## 02: Onboarding
- #3 Current role is asked of switchers only.
- #4 The plan shows weeks ("about 14 weeks") with an optional "show dates" toggle.

## 03: Day-1 case (first 15 minutes)
- #5 The limit stays at 3 questions.
- #6 Order: case, then sign-up, then onboarding, then home.

## 04: Home and skill map
- #7 Bottom bar keeps 5 items; Career shows a light preview until Stage 2.
- #8 The skill map sits second, under the next-action card.

## 05: Concept block
- #9 The Attempt can be skipped with "I don't know"; it is logged.
- #10 The "when it misleads" line stays.

## 06: Quiz with confidence
- #11 Confidence stays, labelled Guessing / Fairly sure / Very sure, with the percentage small.

## 07: Tree builder
- #13 Tap-and-place only at launch; no free typing of nodes.
- #14 Appears in M3 and M6, plus an optional, ungraded tool inside cases.

## 08: Case inbox and brief
- #15 Choose the variant first; switching later resets hints and asks.
- #16 The Professional variant is shown locked (Plus) on Free.

## 09: Stakeholder chat (scripted)
